Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

This policy explains how DrDlamini.com handles information when you visit this personal and professional website.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and experience. Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose Accept in the analytics consent banner.

When enabled, analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate geographic area, device and browser information, referral source, session information, and interactions with the site. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies or similar technologies to distinguish visits and sessions.

Your analytics choice

You can accept or decline analytics. If you decline, this site does not load the Google Analytics library. Your choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember it. You can reopen the consent controls at any time by selecting Cookie Preferences in the footer.

Information you choose to provide

This site currently does not use a web form to collect personal information. If you choose to contact me through an email link, the information you send is handled through your email provider and the recipient's email service.

Third-party content and links

The site contains links to third-party websites and includes an embedded YouTube video using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. Visiting or interacting with third-party content may result in those services receiving information under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and sale of information

This site does not intentionally use analytics data for targeted advertising and does not intentionally sell personal information collected through the site.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website, analytics practices, or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the most recent update.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to GQ@deeplyoptimistic.com.
